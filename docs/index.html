Three roles

Role 01

Mission Designer

Plot waypoints on the map, publish the job, review incoming bids and award it to one pilot.

Pilot

What's inside

Everything a mission needs, end to end

Map planning

Waypoints & geofence

Draw the route node by node on a Leaflet map and fence the flight zone with a circle or polygon — path length and estimated flight time update live.

Bidding

Competitive bids

Pilots bid in USD with an optional message; the designer compares offers side by side and awards exactly one — the rest are rejected automatically.

Lifecycle

Status all the way

Every mission walks the same six-step ladder from draft to completed, with cancellation handled cleanly at any point along the way.

Notifications

In-app + email

New bids, awards, rejections, cancellations and overdue flights all land in the bell menu — and go out as emails when mail is enabled.

Ratings

Both sides rate

After completion the designer and the pilot rate each other, 1–5 with a comment, and the score follows the profile around the marketplace.

Admin

Moderation & audit

Admins see platform-wide stats, hide or remove missions, suspend users — and every action lands in an append-only audit log.
